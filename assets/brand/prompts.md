# Kiekkopolku: logoluonnosten generointi

Menetelmä: sisäänrakennettu image_gen-työkalu. Molemmat kuvat ovat hyväksyttäväksi tarkoitettuja luonnoksia. Täyden logon generoinnissa kuvakeluonnosta käytettiin symbolin referenssinä; logoa ei ole vielä hyväksytty.

## Sovelluskuvake

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE square app-icon concept for Finnish disc golf history/map application Kiekkopolku. Only the symbol, NO text. Flat solid fresh nature green (#31965D) unified rounded map-location pin with negative white space forming a minimalist recognizable disc golf basket: broad top band, just two simple sloping supports suggesting the catching assembly, central pole and shallow catching tray. The basket must read as disc golf rather than basketball. The pin and basket form ONE coherent geometric symbol. Nordic, modern, friendly, calm, clean and trustworthy outdoor identity. Extremely simple silhouette, strong at 48px. White background filling the square, no rounded-square boundary or mockup. Center ALL green geometry inside the central 60% of the square to allow Android adaptive icon safe area. No shadows, gradients, 3D, detailed chains, mascots, esports styling, watermarks or extra objects. This is an approval concept, not a packaged Android resource.

## Täysi logo
